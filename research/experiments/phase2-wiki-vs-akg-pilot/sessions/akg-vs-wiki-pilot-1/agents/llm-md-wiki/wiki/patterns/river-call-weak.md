# River Call with Weak Hand

**Count: 1/1**

Villain called a river bet with a marginal/weak made hand at showdown.

## Evidence
- [[hands/hand-2]]: Board 4h Th Ad 3h 7s — villain held 6d 4c (pair of 4s, bottom pair). Hero bet 2 into pot on river; villain called. Called with bottom pair on a board with an ace, flush draw possible, and four overcards to villain's pair.

## Notes
- Single sample. Suggests villain may be a reluctant folder on the river — worth probing with value bets and bluffs.
- Cross-reference [[patterns/flop-cbet]]: villain was passive all streets before calling river.
