# Hand 3

**Hero:** BB — Ts 2s
**Villain:** SB
**Board:** 5c Ks Qc (flop only)

## Action
- Preflop: Villain completes SB (call 1), hero checks BB. No raise — villain is the preflop *caller*.
- Flop (5c Ks Qc): Hero checks. Villain bets 3 (into pot of ~4, ~75% pot). Hero folds.

## Revealed
- No showdown. Hero folded Ts 2s (no piece of K-Q-5 board).

## What It Reveals
- Villain bet the flop as the preflop *caller* after hero checked — this is a probe/float bet, not a c-bet.
- Board (5c Ks Qc) is semi-coordinated (two broadway + flush draw). Villain may bet here for value or semi-bluff, or simply take advantage of a free-to-steal spot.
- Adds evidence that villain is willing to be aggressive on the flop even without preflop initiative; pairs with [[patterns/flop-cbet]] where villain also bet draw-heavy/connected boards.
