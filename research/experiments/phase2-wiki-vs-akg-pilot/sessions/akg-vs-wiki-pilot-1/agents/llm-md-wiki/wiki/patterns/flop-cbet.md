# Flop C-Bet

**Count: 1/2** (c-bet opportunities only; hand-3 excluded — villain was preflop caller)

Villain c-bets the flop as the preflop aggressor — tracked only when villain had preflop initiative.

## Evidence
- [[hands/hand-1]]: Ts 9d 6s (draw-heavy) — villain bet ~75% pot after hero check. **C-bet YES.**
- [[hands/hand-2]]: 4h Th Ad (dry/ace-high) — villain checked flop with bottom pair (6d 4c). **C-bet NO.**

## Probe/Float Bets (villain as preflop caller)
- [[hands/hand-3]]: 5c Ks Qc — villain bet ~75% pot after hero checked, despite being preflop caller. Shows willingness to take aggression without initiative on draw-heavy/broadway boards.

## Notes
- Small sample (2 c-bet opportunities). Board texture appears relevant: bet connected/draw-heavy boards, checked dry ace-high board.
- Villain also bets as caller on coordinated boards — generally flop-aggressive on wet textures.
- When villain does not c-bet, they may still call river bets with weak holdings — see [[patterns/river-call-weak]].
